This module extends ``salesperson_transfer_portfolio`` to transfer
salesperson visits in ``draft`` or ``confirm`` states, tracking
salesperson changes and recording transferred visits in the transfer
registry.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

To transfer salesperson visits:

1. Open the **Transfer Customer Portfolio** wizard to transfer a
   salesperson's portfolio, including visits.
2. Alternatively, select contacts or opportunities and use the
   corresponding **Transfer** action.
3. Select the new salesperson and review the records to be transferred.
4. Click **Transfer** to complete the process.

Only visits in ``draft`` or ``confirm`` states are transferred. Visited,
cancelled, and incident visits remain unchanged.

Transferred visits are recorded in the transfer registry, and
salesperson changes are tracked in the visit chatter. Associated
calendar events are also updated with the new salesperson.
