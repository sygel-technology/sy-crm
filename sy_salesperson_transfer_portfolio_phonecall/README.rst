This module extends ``salesperson_transfer_portfolio`` to transfer CRM
phone calls in ``open`` or ``pending`` states, tracking salesperson
changes and recording transferred calls in the transfer registry.

Usage
=====

To transfer CRM phone calls:

1. Go to **CRM > Sales > Transfer Customer Portfolio** to transfer a
   salesperson's portfolio, including phone calls.
2. Alternatively, select contacts or opportunities and use the
   corresponding **Transfer** action.
3. Select the new salesperson and review the records to be transferred.
4. Click **Transfer** to complete the process.

Only phone calls in ``open`` or ``pending`` states are transferred.
Completed and cancelled calls remain unchanged.

Transferred phone calls are recorded in the transfer registry, and
salesperson changes are tracked in the phone call chatter.
